Privacy Policy for "Makan Apa? - Kita Putar Je"

This Privacy Policy describes how Axworld ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile application and progressive web application Makan Apa? - Kita Putar Je (the "App").

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We respect your privacy. The App does NOT require user account registration, does NOT collect personal identification information (such as your full name, phone number, or biometric data), and does NOT track your personal browsing activity across third-party apps.

2. Device Location Data

The App may request access to your device's approximate or precise location (GPS) solely to calculate distances to nearby eateries or display relevant zones. Your location data is processed locally on your device in real-time and is NEVER stored, transmitted, or sold to our servers or third parties.

3. Voluntary User Submissions (Crowdsourcing)

If you voluntarily submit new eatery suggestions or issue reports via our in-app forms, the data you submit (such as eatery name, general area, and optional contact info) is transmitted securely via HTTPS to our administrative database for verification purposes only.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

The App provides direct navigation links to external map services (such as Google Maps or Waze). We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you visit through our App.

5. Advertising & Analytics

The App is completely ad-free. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising networks.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
